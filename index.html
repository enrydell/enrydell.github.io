About Me

I am a Senior iOS Developer and Full-Stack Engineer with a strong track record of designing and delivering high-impact, scalable applications. Throughout my career, I have thrived at the intersection of robust backend infrastructure and intuitive user experiences, with a particular focus on the demanding environments of the healthcare and financial sectors.

My core expertise lies deeply within the iOS ecosystem. Utilizing Swift, SwiftUI, and UIKit, I build mobile applications that are not only performant but also highly accessible. Working extensively on healthcare platforms—such as Afya Whitebook and multiple applications for Allina Health—has ingrained in me a rigorous approach to data security, precision, and user-centric design that directly impacts patient care and provider workflows.

Beyond mobile client development, I possess robust full-stack capabilities. I architect and implement scalable backends and APIs utilizing technologies like Node.js and Ruby on Rails. My experience extends to building secure, resilient systems for critical banking infrastructure, where ensuring absolute data integrity, transaction safety, and high availability is paramount.

At the heart of my work is a deep commitment to clean architecture, scalable design patterns, and continuous learning. Whether I am exploring modern API paradigms like GraphQL, engineering custom graphics engines from scratch, or optimizing backend performance, my ultimate goal is always to deliver elegant, maintainable solutions to complex technical challenges.
