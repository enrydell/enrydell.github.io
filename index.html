About Me

I am a Senior iOS Developer and Full-Stack Software Engineer with extensive experience designing, building, and deploying mission-critical mobile applications and backend services. Over the years, I have specialized in native Apple ecosystems (Swift, SwiftUI, UIKit) alongside modern backend technologies like Node.js, TypeScript, and SQL databases.

My professional journey includes architecting large-scale mobile applications in demanding industries such as healthcare and banking. I've contributed to flagship iOS platforms like Afya Whitebook—Brazil's primary medical decision app—as well as the suite of digital healthcare apps for Allina Health, focusing on offline data synchronization, modular design patterns (MVVM, VIPER, Clean Architecture), and automated CI/CD pipelines.

Beyond commercial app development, I am deeply invested in software architecture and low-level computer graphics. In my personal projects, I regularly experiment with building custom HTTP frameworks, 3D software rasterizers from scratch, and 2D/3D game systems using Unity and C++. I believe that understanding underlying computer science fundamentals makes for better abstractions in high-level software engineering.

When I'm not coding or refining architecture patterns, I enjoy exploring real-time graphics optimizations, managing my technical ventures, and diving into immersive video games.
